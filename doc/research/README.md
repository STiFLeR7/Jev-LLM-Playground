## How to read the evidence

Official documentation describes the published API contract; it does not establish measured performance in this workspace. Vendor benchmark claims are labeled as such. Community demos are leads for experiments, not independently reproduced results. Proposed use cases are our recommendations.

Sources were accessed or searched on the research date. X returned HTTP 403 for the six original posts linked in the community notes; summaries and displayed publication dates come from the explicitly linked secondary indexes. No engagement counts are retained because they change and do not validate claims.

Notes are original summaries with source links, not full copies of articles or tweet threads. Prices, model aliases, limits, and SDK requirements should be checked again before implementation.

The follow-up collection focuses on Reddit and AI news about Jev/TypeSafe; no further X access was attempted. Reddit pages were directly readable through the public research browser, although some comments and absolute timestamps were unavailable. News coverage is a curated launch-week collection, not a measured ranking of all trending AI stories. Retrieval details are recorded in each file.
