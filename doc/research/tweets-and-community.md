## Broader launch discussion

[Hacker News launch thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49717558) was directly readable. The discussion distinguishes valid output types from correct judgments, questions broad speed comparisons against generative models, and asks how much automation is possible at a target accuracy. A commenter using the founder's handle, CompleteSkeptic, explicitly acknowledges the possibility of confidently wrong answers. These are useful evaluation questions, not substitutes for measurements.

## Research limitations

This is a selected collection, not a complete tweet archive or sentiment study. No authenticated X access was used. Videos, linked demo applications, author identities, and performance claims were not independently tested. Avoid describing these summaries as directly verified tweets in future publications.
